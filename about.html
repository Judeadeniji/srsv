The Central Bank.

The central bank issues $STANDARD into an LP with a Uniswap V4 hook that governs the monetary policy of the token per epoch.


The hook tracks one main metric: net ETH flow. Not wash-tradable volume or botted engagement. Only real capital entering and leaving.

Positive flow → expansion

If net capital flow is positive, the central bank goes into expansion for that epoch. The issuance of $STANDARD runs at a higher rate to incentivize more growth.

Trading fees are then directed to the expansion vault, which purchases tokenized goldand increases protocol owned liquidity to strengthen the reserves for $STANDARD.

fees→expansion vault→gold + liquidity

Negative flow → contraction

If net capital flow is negative, the central bank goes into contraction for that epoch. The issuance rate is decreased to prevent further dilution.

Trading fees are then directed away from the expansion vault and directed to the contraction vault, which is fully used for token buybacks and burns to defend $STANDARD.

fees→contraction vault→buyback + burn

It all begins with charters.

A bank charter is a soulbound NFT that serves as the center of the economy. It's a license to run your own bank and receive $STANDARD issuance. The genesis issue will be 1,000 Founding Charters. Afterwards, new charters will be auctioned in ETH, at a pace set by monetary policy.

Bankers grow by buying expansion licenses for new branches. These licenses are paid in $STANDARD, and fully burned. New charters change who splits the pie, but never forcefully grow the pie itself.

Expansion through branches.

The holders of bank charters can purchase expansion licenses to open additional bank branches to expand their ownership of $STANDARD emissions. Each bank charter can hold 10 total bank branches per charter.

Bank expansion licenses are limited and issued daily at a dutch auction, paid fully in $STANDARD and fully burned. The dutch auction lets every license find its fair market price, with the number of expansion licenses dictated by the central bank’s monetary policy.


Every epoch, the central bank issues a fixed amount of $STANDARD to all bank branches, divided pro rata.

Inflation and Deflation.

$STANDARD is issued to banks throughout the economy on every epoch. It can then be freely used within the economy to purchase expansion licenses.

To remove tokens from this economy, bankers have to incur an equal and opposite reaction that offsets any inflation. The only way for bankers to withdraw their accumulated production is by dissolving the relevant bank branches. Every destroyed branch increases the issuance share for remaining banks.

Removing the final branch burns the bank charter itself and permanently removes that bank from any issuance from the central bank. The only way back in is to win a new charter at auction.

A bank run, inverted.

Every withdrawal mints $STANDARD into the float. This inflation is combatted through the resolution fee.

The resolution fee starts near zero on a quiet day and climbs steeply in a full run. Half of the charged fee is burned. The other half is paid to the bankers who stayed.

In the old world, a run drains the system and the slowest one out eats zero. Here, the people sprinting for the door fund those who refused to move.

The Flywheel.

01

Adoption

Every new charter is paid for using ETH. The treasury takes in hard assets that strengthen and defend $STANDARD.

02

Expansion

Charters expanding banks permanently shrinks the float. Emissions are met with burns to control runway inflation.

03

Fees

Every trade deepens protocol liquidity, stacks the hard reserve in surplus, and funds buybacks in deficit. Volume in either direction feeds the bank.

04

Monetary policy

Policy is decided in real-time and reacts accordingly. If capital leaves: the rate cuts, fees flip into buy-and-burn, and the exit fee adjusts for the exit demand. The system gets more defensive the worse things get.

The float gets scarcer while the balance sheet gets harder.
